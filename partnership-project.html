Frequently Asked Questions

How does the partnership work?
You take a slot, we establish and manage the Okra and Plantain farm operations, and returns are tied to the structured production and harvest cycle.
What is the minimum investment?
The minimum slot is 1 acre, with a total startup cost of ₦710,600 under the Regular Package. You can also acquire more than one acre if you want to scale up.
When do I start earning?
Okra is designed for Year 1 quick returns, while Plantain supports continuous income from years 2 to 5.
Is it 100% tax-free?
The offer is positioned as a 100% tax-free, government-approved structure as stated in the project benefits.
Can I visit my farm?
Yes. Investor visitation rights are part of the trust framework for the project.
What happens after 5 years?
At the end of the 5-year cycle, the partnership closes based on the agreed structure and you can review the next participation step with the company.
How do I sell the produce?
You can sell the produce yourself or Greater One Agro can support sales on your behalf.
